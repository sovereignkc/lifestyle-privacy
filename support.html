Quick Troubleshooting

  • Notifications not appearing: Check iOS Settings → Notifications → LifeStream and verify Allow Notifications is enabled.
  • Restore Subscription: Open LifeStream Settings and tap Restore Purchases to sync your $1.99/month Pro access.
  • Manage or Cancel Subscription: Open the iOS Settings app → tap your Apple Account / Name at the top → tap Subscriptions → select LifeStream → tap Cancel Subscription (at least 24 hours before renewal).
  • BYOK API Key: Paste a valid Google AI Studio Gemini API key in the masked API Key field; keys are stored locally on your device.
