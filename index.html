Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

LifeStream ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to keep your personal habit logs, schedules, and analytics private on your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your known blunders, daily schedules, notification history, and Interception Success Rate analytics are stored locally on your iPhone. If you use Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) mode, your personal Gemini API key is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party AI Processing (Gemini API)

When using the free on-device mode, schedule parsing and notifications run 100% locally without sending text off your device. When using LifeStream Pro or BYOK AI features, the schedule and habit text you submit is sent via encrypted HTTPS directly to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your tripwire notifications. We do not sell, rent, or use your inputs to train external models.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

LifeStream Pro subscriptions ($1.99/month) are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your credit card or Apple ID billing details.

4. Data Deletion

Because your logs and optional BYOK API key reside locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing your logs inside the app or uninstalling LifeStream.
